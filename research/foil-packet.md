# FOIL Packet — Flock / ALPR Records (Hudson Valley)

*Prepared for After Incarceration (afterincarceration.org). Requester: Jose Pineda,
jose.pineda@afterincarceration.org. New York Freedom of Information Law (Public Officers
Law, Article 6, §§84–90).*

> **Before sending:** Confirm each agency's current **Records Access Officer (RAO)** and
> whether they use an **online FOIL portal** (many NY municipalities do — check the agency
> website's "FOIL" page). Mailing addresses below are starting points; verify, and prefer
> email/portal where available so you get a timestamped acknowledgment.

## A. Reusable template (any agency)

> **To:** Records Access Officer, [AGENCY] — [ADDRESS]
> **From:** Jose Pineda, After Incarceration — jose.pineda@afterincarceration.org
> **Date:** [DATE]
> **Re:** FOIL request — Flock Safety / Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) records
>
> Pursuant to the New York Freedom of Information Law (Public Officers Law, Article 6,
> §§84–90), I request copies of the following records concerning automated license plate
> readers operated by or for [AGENCY]:
>
> 1. The current contract/agreement with Flock Safety, Inc. (or any ALPR vendor), including all amendments, renewals, and statements of work.
> 2. All invoices, purchase orders, and payments to the vendor from January 1, 2023 to the present.
> 3. Any grant applications and awards (including NYS DCJS Law Enforcement Technology grants and any federal grants) used to fund the cameras.
> 4. The number and locations (addresses and/or coordinates) of all ALPR cameras operated by or for the agency.
> 5. The agency's ALPR use-and-data policy, including the data-retention schedule.
> 6. Records identifying every agency, jurisdiction, and state with which the agency's ALPR data is shared, and the current network/sharing configuration.
> 7. Audit logs and "Flock transparency"/network-audit reports showing searches of the agency's cameras for the past 24 months, including any searches by or on behalf of federal agencies (including ICE and CBP).
> 8. All communications between the agency and Flock Safety (or the vendor) regarding federal, immigration, or out-of-state access, from January 1, 2025 to the present.
> 9. All governing-body resolutions, meeting minutes, and legal memoranda regarding the ALPR contract and any proposals to cancel or amend it.
>
> I request records in electronic format. As this request is in the public interest and not
> for commercial use, I ask that any fees be waived; if fees will exceed $25, please notify
> me first. Please acknowledge this request within five business days as required by law.
>
> Sincerely,
> Jose Pineda — After Incarceration — jose.pineda@afterincarceration.org
