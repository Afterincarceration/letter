# The Hudson Valley's Flock Dragnet — One-Page Explainer

*After Incarceration · afterincarceration.org · sources in `newburgh-flock-dossier.md`*

## The short version
Newburgh and its neighbors have quietly built a **regional automated-license-plate-reader
(ALPR) surveillance network** through the company **Flock Safety**. Newburgh alone runs
**80+ cameras**; zoom out across the mid-Hudson and crowdsourced mapping shows **~170+
cameras** blanketing the region. These cameras photograph and time-stamp **every** passing
vehicle — not suspects, everyone — and feed a **national** searchable network.

## Why it matters here
- **Sanctuary in name only.** Newburgh is a "Safe and Welcoming City," but Flock data can
  reach federal immigration enforcement three documented ways: **front door** (direct
  data-sharing), **back door** (federal searches that surface in audit logs), and **side
  door** (local officers searching on ICE's behalf). Nationwide, **4,000+** immigration-
  related lookups have been reported.
- **A regional net beats a local promise.** Even if Newburgh locks its own door, its data is
  shared into a network of neighboring systems that may not have sanctuary policies. The
  exposure is the *network*, not any single camera.
- **Public money, private surveillance.** Newburgh paid Flock **$274,400 in 2024**, funded by
  a **NYS DCJS grant**. Grant money doesn't make it free — the civil-liberties cost lands on
  residents.

## What we don't yet know (and are demanding)
- Exactly **which agencies and states** each city shares data with.
- Whether **ICE or CBP** have searched these cameras — the **audit logs** would show it.
- The **full list of municipalities** under contract, and the terms.
*(All of this is the subject of FOIL requests now going out across the Hudson Valley.)*

## What we're asking for
1. **Recorded votes** by local councils on whether to keep these contracts.
2. **Public release of data-sharing settings and audit logs.**
3. **An end to contracts** that can't guarantee data won't reach federal immigration agents.
